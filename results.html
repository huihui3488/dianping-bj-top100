榜单

150 家候选先过评论数门槛，再按双维度加权排序，最后按品牌去重，每家留一席。 结果是 100 家店、100 个品牌，覆盖 47 个细分品类、9 个菜系、48 个商圈。

All 150 candidates pass a review-count threshold, are ranked on two weighted dimensions, then deduplicated by brand down to one seat each. What survives is 100 restaurants, 100 brands, spread over 47 fine-grained categories, 9 cuisines and 48 commercial districts.
